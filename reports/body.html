    Targets

    Nothing is assumed. One rate, set by you, multiplied by headcount in scope.

    Units per agent per business day Account target = rate × roster headcount; team target = rate × team headcount; agent target = the rate.
    Count only Production agents in targets When on, Training-phase agents are left out of every headcount used for targets (they still appear in the tables).
    XMC attach target % of answered XM-line entries · leave empty for no XMC target.
