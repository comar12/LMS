05Channel: chat repeats arrive late far more often than call repeats

Calls are 94% of repeats, so calls drive the number. But 46% of chat repeats are invisible on day 1 versus 27% for calls, which points at chat follow-through (confirming the fix, closing the ticket, stating next steps) as a specific coaching gap.

10Definitions and method

AICRShare of contacts that did not generate a repeat contact within the window. AICR = 1 − repeat contacts ÷ contacts received.
1-Day vs 7-DaySame contacts, different repeat windows. 7-Day is always lower because it catches more repeats; it is the mature number for KPI reporting.
Gap (pp)1-Day AICR minus 7-Day AICR, in percentage points: the repeat problem hidden from a 1-day view.
Late repeats / late share7-Day repeats minus 1-Day repeats, and that count divided by 7-Day repeats.
DoD ΔChange versus the previous calendar day, in pp for rates and contacts for volumes.
Period averageVolume-weighted: total repeats ÷ total received over 15 days.
Callback timeframeTime between the original call and the repeat call, from the Distribution export. A separate cut (includes 7+ days); it does not tie exactly to the daily tables.
TargetsNone were supplied. Enter yours at the top to see gap-to-target on the gauges and KPI tiles.
